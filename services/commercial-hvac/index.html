Maintenance & More

Commercial HVAC

Your tenants and customers notice when the HVAC fails. We service, replace and maintain light commercial equipment with minimal disruption to your business.

Get a Free Quote Instant estimate

For a business, a failed rooftop unit isn't just uncomfortable. It can mean a closed dining room, unhappy tenants or spoiled stock. Kelvin Heating & Cooling works on light commercial heating and cooling for offices, retail stores, restaurants, clinics, churches and small industrial buildings, with clear written scopes so owners and property managers can plan and budget.

Commercial services

  • Rooftop units — service, repair and replacement of packaged rooftop units, including curb adapters and crane lifts when needed.
  • Split systems and furnaces — commercial furnaces, split air conditioners and heat pumps in smaller buildings and units.
  • Make-up air and exhaust — coordination for kitchens and spaces with exhaust needs.
  • Ductless systems — server rooms, offices and spaces that need their own cooling.
  • Planned maintenance — scheduled filter changes, coil cleaning, belt and safety checks with written reports for your records.
  • Controls — programmable and networked thermostats to manage schedules across multiple units.

How we work with businesses

  1. Site review. We document equipment, ages, model numbers and condition.
  2. Written scope. Clear options and prices, including what can wait and what can't.
  3. Scheduling around you. Replacements and major work planned for slower hours where possible.
  4. Records. Maintenance and service reports that property managers can keep on file.

Planning ahead for replacements

Rooftop units rarely fail at a convenient time, and replacement equipment for larger units isn't always in stock. We help owners and property managers build a simple equipment list with ages, condition and expected replacement timing, so budgets can be set in advance and replacements scheduled in spring or fall instead of during a heat wave or a cold snap.

Who we work with

Our commercial clients include small business owners who manage their own buildings, property managers responsible for several sites, and landlords with mixed residential and commercial units. Whatever the setup, you get clear scopes, honest timelines and records you can rely on. Ask us about scheduled maintenance for every unit on your property, with one point of contact for service calls.

Many smaller buildings use residential-style equipment, so our furnace repair and AC repair work applies there too. Ask us about maintenance plans for multiple units.

What affects the cost of commercial HVAC

Commercial HVAC costs depend on the equipment type, tonnage, access and the condition of what's there. Rooftop unit replacements may need a crane, curb adapters, new gas and electrical connections and roof work around the curb. After-hours scheduling to avoid disrupting the business adds labor cost. Maintenance contracts are priced by the number and type of units and the visit frequency. We provide written scopes for owners and property managers to review.

Commercial HVAC questions

Do you offer maintenance contracts?

Yes. We set up scheduled maintenance based on your equipment and how the building is used, with written reports after each visit.

Can you replace a rooftop unit without closing my business?

Usually. We plan the lift and changeover for quieter hours and keep the downtime as short as possible.

Do you work with property managers?

Yes. We provide written scopes, equipment lists and service records to make approvals and budgeting easier.
