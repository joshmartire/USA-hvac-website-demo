Cookie Policy

What this website stores in your browser, and what it doesn't.

Effective date: October 22, 2026

Does this website use cookies?

No. This website does not set cookies, and it does not use analytics, advertising pixels or other tracking technologies.

Browser storage we use

The website uses your browser's session storage to remember that the opening animation has already played during your visit, so it isn't shown again on every page. This stores a single value with no personal information, isn't shared with us or anyone else, and is cleared automatically when you close your browser tab. Because it's needed only to provide the website as you'd expect, it doesn't require consent.

Third-party services

When you submit one of our forms, your browser sends it to FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which delivers it to us by email. That service may display its own pages, such as a spam-protection check, and may use cookies on its own domain under its own policies. Our hosting provider may log technical information such as IP addresses for security purposes, as explained in our Privacy Policy.

Managing storage

You can clear or block site data at any time through your browser settings. The website will continue to work, although the opening animation may play again.

Changes

If we add tools that use cookies or similar technologies, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent before those tools run.
