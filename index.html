Inside the system

Five stages.
Zero shortcuts.

  1. 01Return & filter

    Air comes back from the rooms through the return ducts and passes through the filter, where dust and debris are caught before they reach the equipment.

  2. 02Blower

    A variable-speed blower moves the air through the system, ramping up and down quietly instead of slamming on and off.

  3. 03Burners & heat exchanger

    The burners heat a sealed heat exchanger. Combustion gases stay inside it and vent outdoors, so they never mix with the air you breathe.

  4. 04Cooling coil

    In summer, refrigerant in the coil above the furnace pulls heat and humidity out of the air before it's sent back to the rooms.

  5. 05Supply to every room

    Conditioned air travels through balanced supply ducts to every room, with the thermostat keeping the whole house where you set it.

All FAQs

How accurate is the instant system estimate?

It's a ballpark range for a typical replacement, based on the system you choose, your home's size and the efficiency level. Venting changes, ductwork, electrical upgrades and access can change the final price, which is confirmed in writing after a home visit.

Should I repair or replace my furnace or air conditioner?

A common rule of thumb multiplies the system's age by the repair cost. When the result is well above five thousand dollars, replacement is usually worth pricing out. Frequent breakdowns, discontinued refrigerant and rising energy bills point the same way. We'll show you both options.

How long does a furnace or AC installation take?

Most straightforward replacements take one day. New venting routes, duct changes, heat pumps and furnace and air conditioner combinations can take a day or two longer.

What size system do I need?

It depends on your home's heat loss and heat gain: insulation, windows, air leakage, sun exposure and layout, not just square footage. We size equipment on site rather than copying the old unit, because oversized systems short cycle and leave hot and cold spots.
