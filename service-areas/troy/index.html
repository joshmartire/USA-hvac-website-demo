Heating & Cooling in Troy, MI

Troy's neighborhoods are mostly ranches, colonials and tri-levels from the 1960s through the 1980s. Many of these homes are on their second or third furnace, and their ductwork and wiring reflect decades of updates and additions.

Get a Free Quote Instant estimate

Troy homeowners call us for furnace and air conditioner replacements, comfort problems between the levels of tri-level homes, and cooling for additions and homes with hot water heat. In older homes, much of the work is correcting what earlier changes left behind: undersized returns, aging thermostat wiring and shared chimneys.

Troy is also home to a large office and retail corridor with plenty of light commercial equipment. Below you'll find each of our services with information specific to Troy homes and buildings.

Permits and licensing in Troy

In Michigan, installing or replacing a furnace, air conditioner or heat pump generally requires a mechanical permit and inspection, and the work must be done by a licensed mechanical contractor. In Troy, permits are handled by the City of Troy's building department. New electrical circuits are done by a licensed electrician under an electrical permit. We pull the permits that apply to your project.
